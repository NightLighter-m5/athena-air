## What's new

- **One app, with a menu:** Play, Game Center, and Settings. There's no more separate launcher.
- **Automatic engine switching.** Game Center gets the engine it needs for installs and updates, and the app switches back to the game engine when it's done.
- **Automatic Low Power** on battery at 50% or less (30 FPS cap and lighter graphics). You can also choose it under Settings.
- **Resolution matched to your screen** on every launch.
- **Built-in Game Center installer** with region choice (Asia, NA, EU).
- Safety checks: it won't run from the DMG, it detects if the game is already running, and it cleans up after a crash.
- Smaller download. Unneeded engines were removed.

## Install

Drag the app to Applications, open it, then go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**. Full steps are in the README.

## Known issues

- The mouse can stop turning in one direction in battle (a trackpad is fine). A fix is being tested.
- In fullscreen, don't switch apps (Cmd+Tab) or swipe to another desktop, or the game minimizes. Use windowed mode if you need to switch.
