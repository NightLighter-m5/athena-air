Mission planFrom hangar to airborne

Open Flight Deck from the disk image. It walks you through every leg with one button each.

1

Roll out of the hangar

Click Roll out. Flight Deck copies the plane into your Applications folder (about 2 GB). Or drag World of Warplanes onto Applications yourself.

2

Fuel up

Click your region (Asia Europe N. America) to open Wargaming's official site, then download the Windows version (a .exe file). Back in Flight Deck, click Load fuel and choose that file.

3

Ready for takeoff

Click Start engines. Wargaming's installer opens: click through it like on Windows. When it's done, Game Center opens by itself.

4

Get airborne!

Log in to Game Center, install World of Warplanes (about 25 GB) and hit Play. From now on, just open World of Warplanes from Applications. Game Center keeps the game updated.

MaydayQuestions & problems

macOS won't open Flight Deck ("can't be verified" / "damaged")

macOS blocks apps from outside the App Store. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to Flight Deck. Then open it again.

"Do not try to run the Installer if the wrapper is already running something else"

Something from the plane is still running. Click Mayday in Flight Deck: it shuts everything down and resets the plane. Then click Start engines again.

Nothing happens when I open World of Warplanes

Game Center isn't installed yet. Finish legs 2 and 3 in Flight Deck first.

The game froze or crashed and now won't start

Open Flight Deck and click Mayday, then open World of Warplanes again.

Is this safe? Is it legal?

The plane is a standard Wine wrapper (the same technology as Porting Kit and CrossOver). It contains no game files and no account data. You download Game Center and the game from Wargaming yourself.

Do I need Porting Kit, CrossOver or Sikarugir?

No. The Wine engine, settings and fixes are already inside the plane.

Which server can I fly on?

Any of them: pick the server on the game's login screen, the same as on Windows. Download Game Center from the site for the region you play in.

Why is High texture quality greyed out?

World of Warplanes is a 32-bit game, and it decides it can't safely use High textures under this Wine version. Medium looks close, and the HD client isn't worth its extra 24 GB here.

Why is FPS lower than on a Windows PC?

The game uses DirectX 9, which Macs don't have. Wine translates it to OpenGL as you fly, which costs performance: about 3–4× slower than a native Mac app in our tests. The cockpit settings above help a lot.

My MacBook Air gets hot and FPS drops after a while

Fanless Macs slow down when warm. Fly plugged in, on a hard surface or a stand (a cooling pad helps), with Low Power Mode off.

Can Vulkan or Apple's D3DMetal give more FPS?

No. We tested it: DirectX 9 through Vulkan fails or crashes on Mac, and D3DMetal only supports 64-bit DirectX 11/12 games. OpenGL, the default here, is the one that works.

How do I uninstall it?

Drag World of Warplanes from Applications to the Trash. The game, Game Center and settings are all stored inside it, so that removes everything.

What's inside the plane, technically?
  • Wineskin wrapper with the WineskinCX 23.7.1-4 engine (best DirectX 9 performance in Unigine Heaven tests)
  • shcore.dll from Wine 11, so Game Center 26.x starts on this engine
  • OpenGL renderer, trackpad scrolling fix, Game Mode label
  • Microsoft Visual C++ runtimes (redistributable), needed by Game Center and the game
